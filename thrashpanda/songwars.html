Rules

There is no entry fee. One track per person. If an individual sends more than a song, they'll be disqualified, alliases and alteregos are no excuse. You must be active on the Discord server for your participation to be considered We will only listen to the first 60 seconds of each track. In case of wanting to show off a specific part in a song, that'll be responsability of the competitor in how they layout and fix their piece. Songs can only have one vocalist, being that and the whole file what we'l see and compare, beat matters but in this edition we will prize based off the vocalist's habilities, performance and mix, plus how it fits in their track ofc. The Thrash Panda crew may disqualify "ill intentioned" competitors and skip tracks if it's considered under any circunstances In case of sending a video file, the video's contents won't be taken in consideration to evaluate the song. For transparency motives, each competitor must submit their own track in a chosen publicly available Thrash Panda Discord server channel
